Reckon keeps a decision honest while you are still making it.

Write the decision as two or more hypotheses. Under each one, log what you have seen for it and against it, and how far you trust the source. The balance moves as the evidence comes in, so you can see which hypothesis is carrying the weight and which one you were hoping would.

The method is Analysis of Competing Hypotheses, the structured technique intelligence analysts use when the stakes are high and the evidence is thin.

When the pattern of what you logged matches a known bias, Reckon says so. It flags confirmation bias when every entry supports the hypothesis you started with, anchoring when the first number never moved, and availability when the loudest evidence is simply the newest.

Every decision stays on your phone. No account and no cloud.

Built for the decisions you will have to explain later: a hire, a lease, a treatment, a move.
