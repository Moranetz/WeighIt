Stop deciding from your gut. Start deciding from evidence.

Weigh It is a decision-analysis tool for structured thinking. Test hypotheses. Weigh evidence. See your cognitive biases before they see you.

STRUCTURED HYPOTHESIS TESTING
Frame your decision as competing hypotheses. For each one, log evidence for and against. Watch the balance shift as you add data instead of gut feelings.

EVIDENCE WEIGHTING
Not all evidence is equal. Assign weights based on reliability, relevance, and source quality. The tool calculates what your evidence actually supports — not what you wish it supported.

COGNITIVE BIAS VISIBILITY
The app flags when your evidence pattern matches known cognitive biases — confirmation bias, anchoring, availability heuristic. See your blind spots in real-time.

LOCAL & PRIVATE
All your decisions stay on your device. No cloud. No accounts. Your thinking process is nobody's business.

For anyone who makes important decisions and wants to make them better.
